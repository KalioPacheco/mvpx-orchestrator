# Code of Conduct

MVPX welcomes technical disagreement and direct feedback. Contributors are expected to keep discussions focused on the work and treat other participants with respect.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, or intentionally disruptive conduct.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

When raising a conduct concern, avoid posting private or sensitive details publicly. Use a private maintainer channel when one is available.
